How it's constrained

  • 1Fixed componentsThe model fills six fields. It can't add buttons, change the layout or write free-form UI.
  • 2Design tokens, not model choicesThe pillar comes from a fixed list, and the design system maps it to color.
  • 3Length limits in the UIEvery field has a character budget, so a long answer can't break the card.
  • 4A safe path for medical questionsSymptoms or treatment questions get a referral card, not advice.
  • 5A designed failure stateIf the output doesn't fit the contract, you see a friendly fallback, not a broken card.

Principles · designing with AI

      
      
Point of view

      
How I design with AI

      
Five principles I design by. Each one is running somewhere on this canvas.

      
    
        
  • Show the plan, then act
    The AI guide lists its steps before it moves, and a Stop button is always one tap away.
    • 
        
  • Answer from sources
    Every answer links back to the frames it came from, so you can check the work.
    • 
        
  • Design the system, not the sentence
    The model fills in fields. The design system owns layout, color and limits.
    • 
        
  • Say what you don't know
    Job tours list what this portfolio doesn't show, and the guide sends you to me when it can't answer.
    • 
        
  • Keep people in control
    Anything the AI makes can be stopped, dragged, dismissed or cleared.
